Privacy Policy

Africa Studio (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides FocusShield, an Android application designed to help users manage app usage by locking selected applications for a chosen period. This policy explains information the app accesses, how it is used, and user choices.

1. Information FocusShield accesses

To provide its features, FocusShield may access or process information on your Android device, including:

  • Selected application information: package names of applications you choose to lock and information needed to identify protected applications.
  • App-usage and foreground-app information: Android Usage Stats information and relevant events used to determine when a protected application is opened.
  • Accessibility events: window-state and related accessibility events used to detect when a protected application becomes active and display the lock screen. Depending on Android configuration, the service may be permitted to retrieve window content.
  • Lock and scheduling settings: lock durations, lock end times, scheduled locks, and preferences needed for the app to function.
  • Permission status: whether the Android permissions and special access settings required for app protection are enabled.

FocusShield uses this information to provide app-locking and scheduling features. The app is not intended to read private messages, record calls, or collect unrelated personal information.

2. How information is used

Information described above is used to enforce configured app locks, identify when a protected application is opened, display the lock screen and remaining time, manage scheduled locks, remember settings and lock state, and check whether required permissions are enabled.

3. Storage and sharing

The current version is designed to store app-lock settings and scheduled-lock information locally on your device, using Android local storage and the Room database.

In the version reviewed for this policy, Firebase dependencies and configuration were removed, and unused network permissions were removed. FocusShield does not provide an account or cloud-sync service in this version.

We do not intend to transmit the app-usage and lock information described in this policy to our own servers or sell it to third parties. This description must be reviewed whenever the app, its dependencies, or its features change. Android, your device manufacturer, or other software on your device may process information under their own privacy practices.

4. Permissions and Accessibility Service

FocusShield may request Usage Access, Accessibility Service access, and other Android permissions needed for specific features. Usage Access helps identify which application is active. Accessibility access helps detect relevant window events and display the lock screen when a protected application is opened.

You enable these settings through Android system settings. You can decline or revoke access, but app-lock enforcement may not work correctly without the required permissions. The in-app disclosure explains the purpose of Accessibility access before asking you to enable the service.

5. Retention and deletion

Lock settings, preferences, and scheduled-lock records are retained locally for as long as they are needed by the app or until they are removed through normal app operation. Expired locks may be removed as part of lock management.

You can clear FocusShield’s app data or uninstall the app to remove its local app data, subject to Android device behavior and any device-level backup settings. Because this version does not provide a FocusShield account or cloud-sync feature, it does not offer account-based data deletion.

6. Security

FocusShield relies on Android’s application sandbox and local storage mechanisms to protect locally stored information. Keep your device secure and avoid granting permissions to apps you do not trust. No method of storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

FocusShield is a general-purpose app-management tool and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If the app is later directed toward children or included in a family-targeted program, its features and data practices will be reviewed against applicable requirements.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when FocusShield’s features, permissions, or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the Privacy Policy URL linked within the app and on Google Play. The effective date will be updated when appropriate.
